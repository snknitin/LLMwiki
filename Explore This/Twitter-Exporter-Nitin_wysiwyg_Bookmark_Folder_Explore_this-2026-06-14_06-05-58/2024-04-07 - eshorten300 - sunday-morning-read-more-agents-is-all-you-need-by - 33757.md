Sunday morning read: More Agents is All You Need by Junyou Li et al. 

We've all heard about scaling the number of parameters, but what about scaling the number of agents? 

From the paper, "LLM performance may likely be improved by a brute-force scaling up of the number of agents instantiated."

Shown below is the sample-and-vote strategy. Multiple predictions are created by generating an output from the language model -- outputs will vary per call, especially with a high `temperature` value. The generations are then aggregated to find the centroid answer based on the similarity score.
