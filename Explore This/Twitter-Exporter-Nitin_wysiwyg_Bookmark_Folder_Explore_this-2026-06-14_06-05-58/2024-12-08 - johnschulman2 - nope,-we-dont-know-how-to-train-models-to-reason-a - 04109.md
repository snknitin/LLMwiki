# Nope, we don't know how to train models to reason about controversial topics from first principles;...

> **Source:** [@johnschulman2](https://x.com/johnschulman2) · 2024-12-08 · 👍 950 · 💬 25 · 🔖 359 · 👁 105668

> 🔗 [View tweet on X](https://x.com/johnschulman2/status/1865580250119504109)

## Tweet Content

Nope, we don't know how to train models to reason about controversial topics from first principles; we can only train them to reason on tasks like math calculations and puzzles where there's an objective ground truth answer. On general tasks, we only know how to train them to imitate humans or maximize human approval. 
Nowadays post-training / alignment boosts benchmark scores, e.g. see 
https://
qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen2.5/
