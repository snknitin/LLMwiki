# Learning Digest Template

Copy the structure into Learning or Competitions and replace the placeholders.

```markdown
---
title: Clear topic name
kind: learning
topics: NLP, evaluation
status: ready
updated: 2026-09-26
minutes: 20
---
# Clear topic name

**Next action (2 minutes):** One concrete action.

## Five-minute digest
Three ideas and why they matter.

## Work through one example
An original example, assumptions and a small exercise.

## Go deeper
Explanation tied to the cited evidence.

## Check yourself
Three retrieval questions and an explicit finish condition.

## Sources and limitations
Author, title, URL, publication date when known, checked date, uncertainty.
```
