## Gemma paper-track scout — 60 minutes maximum

1. **10 minutes:** read the evaluation criteria and identify one research question.
2. **15 minutes:** specify a simple baseline and one change to test.
3. **20 minutes:** check data rights, compute needs and whether results can be reproduced.
4. **15 minutes:** save a one-paragraph enter/defer decision with scope and remaining work.

The official page lists $15,000 for best paper, $10,000 for a new resource and $10,000 for a new application. It asks for a submitted research write-up; an unsubmitted draft is not considered. Attached private Kaggle resources may become public after the deadline, so inspect the exact submission contents before publishing. The track explicitly does not award points or medals. [Official paper-track page](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/gemma-4-developer-agent-paper).

**An original project hypothesis, not an organizer endorsement:** a small reproducible benchmark of local coding-agent failures could fit the user's existing infrastructure. It would need a genuine research contribution and evidence against the actual judging criteria. Merely wrapping an existing model or dashboard would not establish that contribution.
