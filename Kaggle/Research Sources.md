## Rank verification rules

1. Capture the competition slug and exact source URL.
2. Preserve author-declared rank, Kaggle write-up rank metadata and final-leaderboard rank as separate fields when available.
3. Use final private rank for final-placement comparisons; do not substitute public rank.
4. Mark conflicts or missing bodies; do not convert them into verified solution summaries.
5. Record the retrieval date separately from publication and competition deadline dates.

Real examples motivated this rule: a title claiming first place in another competition appeared with seventeenth-place page metadata; ROGII has a public-eighth/private-eighty-sixth write-up. These were excluded from the top-ten method collection.
