# Kindle Collections — draft plan v1

Generated 2026-10-06 from `kindle_library.md` (1011 books). One book may appear under several collections (Kindle allows that).

## How to apply on your device (each collection ~2–4 min)
1. Kindle app (phone or e-ink) → **My Content** → tap **+ / Manage collections** → create the collection by name.
2. In the library search box, paste a title from the list; long-press the book → **Move to collection → <name>**.
3. A book can sit in multiple collections; re-doing a move doesn't duplicate the book, it re-tags it.
4. Collections sync to every device signed into your Amazon account.

Tip: build collections on the **phone app** first (touch long-press is faster than the e-ink) — they appear on the e-ink automatically.
Only create the collections you'll actually browse; ~8–12 beats 25 small ones.
