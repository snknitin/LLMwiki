# The ML/AI interview pattern has changed in 2026. The interview bar has shifted — from understanding...

> **Source:** [Manish Mazumder](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAACSuFsMBOazTIsYXLRIeGQi1y0x70tcnwOM) · ML Research Engineer • IIT Kanpur CSE • LinkedIn Top Voice 2024 • NLP, LLMs, GenAI, Agentic AI, Machine Learning · 2w

> [View post on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7483174635556204544/)

## Post

The ML/AI interview pattern has changed in 2026. The interview bar has shifted — from understanding LLMs to building AI agents and systems around them. That's why the old preparation roadmap is no longer enough. Here's the interview roadmap you should follow:

𝟭. 𝗖𝗹𝗮𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗰𝘀 𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗹𝗹 𝗺𝗮𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗿 (𝗱𝗼𝗻'𝘁 𝘀𝗸𝗶𝗽)
- Bias-variance, gradient descent, regularization, evaluation metrics.
- Every interview still opens here. This is your filter round.
𝟮. 𝗟𝗟𝗠 𝗳𝘂𝗻𝗱𝗮𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗮𝗹𝘀
- Attention, tokenization, fine-tuning vs prompting, context windows, MoEs.
- If you can't explain why context length affects cost and latency, this is a gap.
𝟯. 𝗥𝗔𝗚 (𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗼𝗹𝗱 "𝗻𝗲𝘄" 𝘁𝗼𝗽𝗶𝗰)
- Chunking strategies, embedding tradeoffs, retrieval evaluation.
- Still asked. Still puzzling candidates.
𝟰. 𝗔𝗴𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗳𝘂𝗻𝗱𝗮𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗮𝗹𝘀 (𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗻𝗲𝘄 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗹 𝘁𝗼𝗽𝗶𝗰)
- Tool use, planning loops, memory, single-agent vs multi-agent design.
- This is where most candidates go blank right now.
𝟱. 𝗢𝗿𝗰𝗵𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 & 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝘁𝗼𝗰𝗼𝗹𝘀
- MCP, A2A, why a "puppeteer" pattern beats one giant do-everything agent.
- Interviewers use this to test if you understand systems, not just models.
𝟲. 𝗘𝘃𝗮𝗹𝘂𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 & 𝗳𝗮𝗶𝗹𝘂𝗿𝗲 𝗺𝗼𝗱𝗲𝘀
- How do you know an agent actually worked?
- Hallucination, tool-call errors, cost blowouts.
- This is the "senior" signal question.
𝟳. 𝗦𝘆𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗺 𝗱𝗲𝘀𝗶𝗴𝗻 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗰 & 𝗟𝗟𝗠 𝘀𝘆𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗺𝘀
- State management across agent calls, retry/fallback logic, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, observability for multi-step chains.
- Plus serving at scale: caching, batching, model routing (small vs frontier model per task), rate limits, cost-per-request tradeoffs.
- "Design an agentic pipeline" has quietly replaced "design a recommendation system" as the go-to senior-level whiteboard question.
𝟴. 𝗖𝗼𝘀𝘁 & 𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝘆 𝘁𝗿𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗼𝗳𝗳𝘀
- When to use a small model vs a frontier one in an agent pipeline.
- Companies are burning budgets on this in production — they want engineers who think about it.

The honest truth: the fundamentals didn't disappear. They just got a new layer on top — and that layer now includes system design for agents, not just models.
