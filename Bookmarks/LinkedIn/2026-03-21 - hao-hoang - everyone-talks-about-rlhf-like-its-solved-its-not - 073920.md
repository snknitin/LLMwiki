𝘌𝘷𝘦𝘳𝘺𝘰𝘯𝘦 𝘵𝘢𝘭𝘬𝘴 𝘢𝘣𝘰𝘶𝘵 𝘙𝘓𝘏𝘍 𝘭𝘪𝘬𝘦 𝘪𝘵'𝘴 "𝘴𝘰𝘭𝘷𝘦𝘥." 𝘐𝘵'𝘴 𝘯𝘰𝘵.

Today we have:
- REINFORCE
- PPO
- GRPO
- GSPO
- CISPO
- RLOO

And 90% of engineers still can't answer:

"Which one should I actually use?"

Most resources explain what these algorithms are.

Almost none explain:
- when they break
- why they behave differently
- what tradeoffs actually matter in production

That's why this book by Nathan Lambert is worth your time.

It doesn't try to sell you a "best algorithm."

It maps the design space:
- variance vs stability
- reward model dependency
- memory / compute tradeoffs
- implementation complexity
