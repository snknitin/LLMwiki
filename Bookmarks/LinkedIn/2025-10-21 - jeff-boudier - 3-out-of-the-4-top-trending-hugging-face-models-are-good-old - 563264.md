## Post

3 out of the 4 top trending Hugging Face models are good old OCR... what's going on?

I think these models hit the sweet spot of AI use cases for companies:
- expense receipts, instruction manuals, PDF extraction, chart analysis, document agents... business cases for every company
- specialized, small OCR models are 100x cheaper to run than large LLMs (and more accurate)
- best models are open and MIT / Apache 2.0 licensed, can be run in-house without breaking the bank

But each model has specific features and strengths, like languages, document types and output formats.

How to select the best model(s) for your use case? 
The Hugging Face team has the answers. In the blog post below, you will learn how to:
📚 pick a model
🏆 compare bleeding-edge ones
🚢 deploy (locally/remotely)
🚀 go beyond OCR!
