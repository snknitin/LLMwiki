## Post

3 days to get fully Anthropic certified. Challenge accepted. 

I just completed all current Anthropic certifications. 

A few thoughts after going through all four. My honest take : 
If your goal is simply to collect badges, doing all four makes sense.
If your goal is to better understand how to design and build agentic systems, I’d prioritize:
1. Architect Foundations
2. Certified Developer
3. Architect Professional
Personally, I’d skip the Associate exam unless you specifically want to complete the entire certification path.
From an exam experience perspective, the Architect Foundations exam was my favorite. It’s structured around several real-world scenarios, with multiple questions tied to each one, which makes the exam feel more engaging than a series of unrelated multiple-choice questions.
In terms of content, though, the Architect Professional is the certification I’d recommend most. It goes deeper into the architectural thinking behind building agentic systems.
Overall, these certifications are still fairly introductory tbh, which is expected, given that three of them are Foundation/Associate level. Even the Professional exam felt more approachable than AWS Professional certifications for instance. I’m curious to see how it evolves over time.
As for preparation, I didn’t do any dedicated studying. My hands-on experience building agentic workloads on AWS was enough to pass. The official exam guides clearly describe the target audience and expected level for each exam/ a target profile, if that's you, you can comfortably take the exam without much prep. However, if you're starting, I'd suggest: 
- Anthropic Skilljar
- Frank Kane's practice exams 
- Anthropic docs 
That said, nothing replaces building real systems. Hands-on experience was by far the most valuable preparation.
