## Post

7 GitHub repos that make you feel unstoppable as an AI engineer!

1. Hands-On Large Language Models

A complete, hands-on codebase that follows the Hands-On Large Language Models book

Includes Jupyter notebooks that cover everything from the basics of LLMs to fine-tuning and deploying them

↳ https://lnkd.in/gpvPemeG

---

2. Awesome MCP Servers

An extensive list of frameworks and tools designed to run modular, scalable AI backends

Great if you’re building agent platforms or ML workflows that need to perform at scale

↳ https://lnkd.in/gVJXuEjq

---

3. Awesome MCP Clients

A curated set of client tools and SDKs to connect seamlessly with MCP-based infrastructures

Perfect for interfacing with AI agents and experimenting with real-time interaction

↳ https://lnkd.in/eitFgbQy

---

4. GenAI Agents

Hands-on tutorials and implementations for building generative AI agents

Covers a range of techniques from simple prompting to full agent architectures

↳ https://lnkd.in/gaiZgzpA

---

5. Made with ML

Practical guidance on shipping ML projects from ideation and experimentation to deployment and iteration

It’s a full-stack view of what it takes to build ML products that work in production

↳ https://lnkd.in/gn2RhT_m

---

6. Prompt Engineering Guide

A growing collection of resources to improve your prompting skills

Includes papers, lectures, templates, and examples

↳ https://lnkd.in/gDt3Sknr

---

7. AI Agents for Beginners

A free 11-lesson course that breaks down the fundamentals of agent-based systems

Beginner-friendly and project-driven

↳ https://lnkd.in/gVm5gmv7
