## Post

We teamed up with Meta to release a free notebook that turns your documents into high-quality synthetic datasets by using Llama! Then we fine-tune with Unsloth AI. 🦙🦥

Free Colab Notebook: https://lnkd.in/eKCUYnhx

What the notebook does:
• Auto-parses PDFs, websites, YouTube videos and more
• Uses Meta’s Synthetic Data Kit + Llama 3.2 to generate QA pairs
• Cleans and filters the data automatically
• Fine-tunes the dataset with Unsloth + Llama
• Fully locally with no API calling necessary

Meta's Synthetic Data Kit repo: https://lnkd.in/em7mjJa4
