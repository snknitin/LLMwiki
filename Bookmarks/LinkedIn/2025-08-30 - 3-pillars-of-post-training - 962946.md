## Post

Pre-training makes a model exist. 

Post-training makes it usable. 

We hear a lot about fine-tuning LLMs, but that’s not the only part of post-training. Post-training techniques like Reinforcement Learning and Test-Time Scaling are becoming increasingly important in providing competitive AI models.

Here’s an overview of the 3 Pillars of Post-Training.

𝟭. 𝗙𝗶𝗻𝗲-𝗧𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴
Fine-tuning adapts pretrained LLMs to specific tasks or domains by updating parameters on curated datasets.
• Enhances performance for tasks (e.g. sentiment analysis, question answering)
• Better captures contextual nuance in domain-specific data (e.g. medical or legal)
• Requires high quality datasets and considerable compute power

𝟮. 𝗥𝗲𝗶𝗻𝗳𝗼𝗿𝗰𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗟𝗲𝗮𝗿𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴 (𝗥𝗟)
RL enhances adaptability and aligns models with human values and preferences by leveraging dynamic feedback.
• Improves a model's helpfulness and safety for conversational tasks
• Reduces undesirable outputs by punishing responses that are unhelpful or unsafe
• Can bake undesirable values or biases into a model

𝟯. 𝗧𝗲𝘀𝘁-𝗧𝗶𝗺𝗲 𝗦𝗰𝗮𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴 (𝗧𝗧𝗦)
TTS optimizes model performance without altering the core architecture.
• Involves generating multiple responses for a single input and choosing the best one
• Improves multi-step reasoning by breaking down complex problems (e.g. Chain-of-Thought and Tree-of-Thought)
• Can slow down inference and make it more expensive (because of more output tokens)

For an excellent, comprehensive guide on these 3 post-training techniques, check out LLM Post-Training: A Deep Dive into Reasoning Large Language Models.
