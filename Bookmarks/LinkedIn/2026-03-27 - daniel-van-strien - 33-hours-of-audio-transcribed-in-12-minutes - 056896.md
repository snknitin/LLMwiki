# 33 hours of audio transcribed in 12 minutes!

> **Source:** [Daniel van Strien](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAABWhaCIB2ZxwYxwePaRDVOYSL1FvGP8n_Es) · Machine Learning Librarian at Hugging Face 🤗 | Making AI work for libraries, archives, and their communities · 4mo

> [View post on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7443327611407056896/)

## Post

33 hours of audio transcribed in 12 minutes!

Cohere Labs just released Cohere Transcribe, a 2B parameter open-source ASR model (Apache 2.0, 14 languages). It's very fast!

To test it, I grabbed 66 episodes of CBS Suspense (1940s radio drama, public domain) from the Internet Archive and ran them on an A100 via Hugging Face Jobs.

161x realtime. The transcripts are clean — proper punctuation, speaker changes, even the vintage ad reads come through! 

What made this particularly smooth: HF Buckets now mount as filesystem volumes in Jobs. The script just reads from /input and writes to /output. No download step, no upload step. The buckets are the filesystem

The whole thing is a single Python script with inline dependencies. One command to run it.

Everything is public if you want to try it:
