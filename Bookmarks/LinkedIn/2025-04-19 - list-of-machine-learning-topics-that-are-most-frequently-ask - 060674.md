📚 ML Interview Guide

🔹 I've been compiling a list of machine learning topics that are most frequently asked in interviews today.

🔹 This guide (🔗 in comments) is written in collaboration with Aman Chadha and consists of:

◦ Popular ML Algorithms along with their pros and cons  
◦ Ways to optimize model performance  
◦ Fundamental AI concepts to understand the broader impact  
◦ Techniques to handle data imbalances and feature scaling  
◦ Best practices for model evaluation and overcoming common pitfalls  
◦ Deep Learning specifics like Batch Normalization and Active Learning  
◦ Practical insights on A/B testing, data drift, and continuous model training  

📈 Deep Dive into Core Topics:

◦ 𝐌𝐚𝐜𝐡𝐢𝐧𝐞 𝐋𝐞𝐚𝐫𝐧𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐁𝐚𝐬𝐢𝐜𝐬: Overview of algorithms like Linear Regression, SVMs, and Decision Trees, with a clear delineation of when to use them based on their strengths and limitations.

- 𝐎𝐩𝐭𝐢𝐦𝐢𝐳𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐌𝐨𝐝𝐞𝐥 𝐏𝐞𝐫𝐟𝐨𝐫𝐦𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞: From setting the right batch size and learning rate schedules to advanced strategies like regularization and dropout to combat overfitting.

- 𝐃𝐚𝐭𝐚 𝐒𝐭𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐠𝐲: How to manage long-tail data, deal with data leaks, and ensure data diversity to make your models robust and fair.

- 𝐃𝐞𝐞𝐩 𝐋𝐞𝐚𝐫𝐧𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐒𝐭𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐠𝐢𝐞𝐬: Detailed discussion on underfitting vs. overfitting, the importance of model architecture, and leveraging advanced techniques like attention mechanisms and transformer models.

𝐅𝐞𝐚𝐭𝐮𝐫𝐞 𝐄𝐧𝐠𝐢𝐧𝐞𝐞𝐫𝐢𝐧𝐠: How to extract the most relevant information from your data to improve model accuracy.

- 𝐃𝐢𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐬𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐚𝐥𝐢𝐭𝐲 𝐑𝐞𝐝𝐮𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧: Techniques like PCA to manage high-dimensional data effectively.

- 𝐇𝐚𝐧𝐝𝐥𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐍𝐨𝐧-𝐒𝐭𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐚𝐫𝐲 𝐃𝐢𝐬𝐭𝐫𝐢𝐛𝐮𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐬: Strategies to manage and adapt to changes in data over time, ensuring your models remain effective.

🚀 𝐏𝐫𝐚𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐥 𝐀𝐩𝐩𝐥𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐬:

- Real-World Examples: From hypothesis testing in marketing to real-time predictions in financial services.
- A/B Testing Pitfalls: Common mistakes and how to avoid them, ensuring statistically sound conclusions in your experiments.
- MLOps Practices: From automating ML pipelines to implementing CI/CD for machine learning projects, ensuring your ML systems are scalable and maintainable.
