## Post

Tired of pulling your hair out tracing matrix dimensions across your code?

My latest article dives into einops, illustrating with examples how it simplifies common tensor manipulations. Learn how to transpose axes, flatten or unflatten dimensions, and rearrange data according to custom patterns. Because einops is designed to be self-documenting, it can help to boost productivity, reduce mistakes, and lower cognitive load.

Since einops already has a great multi-part tutorial covering all the key features, this guide serves to complement the official einops tutorials, not to replace them. While the use of e i n o p s tensors as examples may be useful for fields such as computer vision, I found that I learn better with small matrices as examples, as I could more easily visually trace how numbers in tensors change.

I also have a section dedicated to einsum at the end for completeness.
