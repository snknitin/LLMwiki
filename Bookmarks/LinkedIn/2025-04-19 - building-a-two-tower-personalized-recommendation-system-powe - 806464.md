Building a Two-Tower Personalized Recommendation System Powered by GNNs, LLMs, and RL

Excited to share an innovative Two-Tower Recommendation System that integrates Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), Large Language Models (LLMs), and Reinforcement Learning (RL) for personalized recommendations on Yelp business data! 

🔍 Key Highlights:
 1️⃣ GNNs for graph-based user and item embeddings.
 2️⃣ LLMs (e.g., Sentence Transformers, GPT-2) for contextual understanding.
 3️⃣ RL to optimize recommendations using Chain-of-Thoughts.

💡 Why It’s Exciting:
This system blends graph intelligence with semantic reasoning and creates a robust framework for dynamic, user-focused recommendations. It’s a scalable, adaptable approach for advancing AI-driven personalization.
