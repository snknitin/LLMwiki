## Post

YouTube's RecSys is a box of insights.

What I love about their paper Deep Neural Networks for YouTube Recommendations, is the amount of thought they have put into designing a system instead of making the model fancier.

Any ML engineer can leverage practical ideas like these.

Hence, I have structured my latest article to reflect system/ops decisions instead of modeling details. I discuss

➡ Why structure recommendations as an extreme classification problem?

➡ Why the usual train-val split is not the best way to create a validation set?

➡ How to ensure the model does not exploit the platform's structure?

➡ How YouTube normalizes features for better convergence?

➡ How to think about the objective function for the model to optimize?

➡ How YouTube ensures its data sources are optimal for model learning.

... and more.
