📝 The A-to-Z of Prompt Engineering • http://prompting.aman.ai

- Prompt engineering involves designing inputs that guide #GenAI models, like LLMs/VLMs, to generate specific and effective responses.
- Here's my primer covering 30+ flavors of Prompt Engineering along with their use-cases.

✅ For more AI primers, follow me on X at: http://x.aman.ai
