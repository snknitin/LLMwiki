🚀 Google introduces #LangExtract!
An open-source Python library for extracting structured information from unstructured text — powered by #Gemini.

✨ What LangExtract can do:
✅ Turn messy text into clean, structured data
✅ Trace every extracted insight back to its source
✅ Instantly visualize what was pulled (and how)

Built for devs, researchers, and analysts who want more than just answers — they want traceable, explainable extraction.
