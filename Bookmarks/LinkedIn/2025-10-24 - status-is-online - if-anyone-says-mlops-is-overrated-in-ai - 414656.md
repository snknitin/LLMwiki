𝗜𝗳 𝗮𝗻𝘆𝗼𝗻𝗲 𝘀𝗮𝘆𝘀 𝗠𝗟𝗢𝗽𝘀 𝗶𝘀 𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗿𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗶𝗻 𝗔𝗜,
ask them to deploy just one model to production.
They’ll quickly realize:
Training a model is easy. Deploying, scaling, and maintaining it is real engineering.
Most people struggle with MLOps not because they lack ML skills but because they don’t understand the end-to-end lifecycle:
data → deployment → monitoring → feedback.
It’s not about MLflow or Airflow it’s about reliability, reproducibility, and scalability.
