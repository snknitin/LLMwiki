EXCLUSIVE: OpenAI is launching a worldwide competition to recruit AI researchers. The prize is a full-time job.

On Monday I talked to Mark Chen, OpenAI's chief research officer, about the OpenAI Model Craft Challenge. It's basically a multi-round contest that tasks people with solving the kinds of problems OpenAI's researchers deal with every day. 

The first challenge is called Parameter Golf. You get 16 megabytes of space, eight H100 GPUs, and 10 minutes to train a model. 

I also chatted with Will DePue, who dropped out of college in 2022, learned ML from non-academic sources like Andrej Karpathy's YouTube videos, and trained his first models from his bedroom. He now runs his own research team at OpenAI. Several people on his team are former mathematicians, neuroscientists, and physicists. 

Will told me the whole point of this challenge is finding people who try weird things. If you've been tinkering with models and wondering if you're good enough to do this professionally, OpenAI is literally saying: here's the sandbox, show us what you've got.
