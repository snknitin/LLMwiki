## Post

Your GenAI interview is won (or lost) in the first 15 minutes. Questions and tips!!

Here are 22 real questions you must be ready to answer:

LLM Fundamentals
 1. What does tokenization really mean, beyond just “splitting text”?
 2. Why are decoder-only models dominant today? When would encoder-decoder be a smarter choice?
 3. Explain attention in plain English — where exactly do queries, keys, and values come into play?
 4. Compare sampling methods: top-K, top-P, and temperature. When would you choose each one?

Prompting & Context Engineering
 5. Share an example where a zero-shot prompt failed. How did you fix it?
 6. How do you track versions of prompts and test them for consistency?
 7. What is “context window waste” and how would you reduce it?

Fine-Tuning & Alignment
 8. LoRA vs QLoRA vs PEFT — how do you balance memory, speed, and accuracy?
 9. What are the biggest limitations of RLHF? Give one real failure case.
 10. When would you pick an open-source model over a proprietary one for fine-tuning?

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
 11. Walk me through how embeddings and similarity search actually work.
 12. Pinecone vs Chroma vs Weaviate — which one would you choose and why?
 13. When does hybrid retrieval (vector + keyword) outperform pure vector search?

Agentic AI
 14. Define an AI agent without using buzzwords.
 15. How do you handle tool-use errors inside an agent loop?
 16. What’s the toughest part about coordinating multiple agents?

MLOps / LLMOps
 17. How would you detect and monitor hallucinations in production?
 18. Which metrics matter most in an evaluation pipeline?
 19. Walk me through deploying a GenAI service with FastAPI + Docker + K8s — how do you ensure rollback safety?

Scaling & Risk
 20. If your system blows the budget because of model size, how do you decide on trade-offs — speed, accuracy, or cost?

MCP (Model Context Protocol)
 21. What is MCP, and why does it matter for interoperability across GenAI tools?
 22. How would you design a safe fallback if an MCP-enabled tool fails mid-workflow?

⸻

Self-test: Can you answer these clearly, without hand-waving? If not, that’s your prep gap.
Mini-project: Pick 5 → record 2-minute answers → rewatch. You’ll instantly spot where you sound credible vs vague.
