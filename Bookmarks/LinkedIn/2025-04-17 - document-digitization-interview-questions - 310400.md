## Post

📄🔍💻 Document Digitization Interview Questions asked in top tech companies

Document digitization is the most critical, yet often overlooked, component of the RAG pipeline. In my previous organization, over 99% of the candidates I interviewed could discuss advanced RAG pipelines in-depth, from hybrid search to 27 types of RAG. However, when probed about digitization, the common answer was always limited to using PyMuPDF as a library. It was evident that many were unaware of layout-aware models, a crucial aspect that signifies real, hands-on experience.

Below are 6 questions consistently asked in Gen AI Interviews in top tech companies :
1. What is the best method to digitize and chunk complex documents like annual reports?
2. How do you handle tables during chunking?
3. How do you handle very large tables for better retrieval?
4. How do you handle list items during chunking?
5. How do you build a production-grade document processing and indexing pipeline?
6. How do you handle graphs and charts in RAG?
