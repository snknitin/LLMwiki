## Post

Trace and evaluate any LLM app with just a single decorator!

DeepEval lets you trace and evaluate your LLM apps with component-level evaluations.

Most LLM evaluations treat the system as a black box, comparing only a single input to a final output. That works for simple apps.

But in real‑world pipelines, problems often occur inside the system, such as:

• A retriever returning irrelevant documents
• A tool call failing silently
• An agent making incorrect decisions

Component‑level evaluation solves this by assessing each part of your pipeline. 
Here's how it works:

• Wrap components such as retrievers, generators, or tools with "@observe".
• Attach different metrics to each part.
• Get clear visibility into which component is failing and why.

This helps you quickly identify bottlenecks and refine complex pipelines more effectively.

It’s 100% Open Source.
