📘 BOOK LAUNCH | Indian Real Estate Risk Handbook – Free to Download

I am pleased to share the launch of the Indian Real Estate Risk Handbook – the inaugural publication under Namahaa Legal's Intelligence Series.

The handbook was formally launched by Shri Jayesh Ranjan, IAS, Special Chief Secretary to the Government of Telangana, in the distinguished presence of Shri K. Srinivas, Secretary, Telangana Real Estate Regulatory Authority (TG RERA).

For most people, buying a home or investing in real estate is the single largest financial decision of their lives. However, many buyers continue to suffer avoidable financial loss because they are unaware of the legal, regulatory and practical risks that exist long before a dispute reaches a courtroom.

Having advised numerous buyers and investors over the years, we have seen a common pattern: many disputes could have been prevented through timely legal awareness and informed decision-making.

That realisation led to this handbook.

Developed over several months of research, legal analysis and practical experience, the Indian Real Estate Risk Handbook has been created to help buyers and investors identify risks before they sign, pay or invest.

The handbook covers a wide range of issues, including:

· Property and Environmental Due Diligence.
· Properties Mortgaged with ULBs such as HMDA / GHMC / DTCP, etc.
· Illegal Refuge Area Conversions.
· Notary Registration Risks.
· LRS & BRS Misconceptions.
· Pre-Launch & UDS Schemes.
· Expression of Interest (EOI) Models.
· Builder-Paid EMI Schemes.
· Managed Farmland Investments.
· Development Agreements.
· One-Time Payment (OTP) Schemes.
· Evolution of TG RERA.
· Estate Planning 

Prepared as part of Namahaa Legal's public legal awareness initiative, the handbook is available free of cost to homebuyers, investors, legal professionals and anyone involved in a real estate transaction.

📖 Download your free copy here: https://namahaalegal.com/
 
If you find the handbook useful, I encourage you to share it with your colleagues, clients, friends and family. Widespread legal awareness is one of the most effective forms of consumer protection.
