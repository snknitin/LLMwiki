# AI evals keep showing up in job postings.

## Post

AI evals keep showing up in job postings.

Cursor is hiring a software engineer for agent evaluation and quality. NVIDIA has a product evaluations lead for GenAI. Amazon wants a product manager working "at the intersection of AI evaluation". Lovable wants a PM to own agent quality and feedback loops. DoorDash is hiring an infrastructure engineer focused on evals.

Evals are also unusually hard to teach yourself. With most AI skills, you can build something and iterate until you get good. With evals, it's hard to get reps before you're doing it on a real product. And a coding agent will write evals for you by default that look reasonable and are plain wrong.

Meanwhile, hardly anyone teaches this. I keep hearing from engineers and PMs that evals questions are where they fail in interviews for AI roles. It's a technical skill, but everybody can learn it.

This is the problem our course, AI Evals for Engineers & PMs, exists to solve. Shreya Shankar and I have taught it to 4,500+ engineers and PMs from teams like OpenAI, Google, Meta, Amazon, and Microsoft, and we rebuilt it around agents this year.
