# I am flying to San Francisco. This is the reading list for my 12-hour flight on AI Evals, Knowledge...

> **Source:** [Paul Iusztin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAACQFQWgBqowPZBqBQgSC3ATmuatVfZkf6fE) · Senior AI Engineer • Founder @ Decoding AI • Author @ LLM Engineer’s Handbook ~ I ship AI products and teach you about the process. · 10mo

> [View post on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7386802940172308480/)

## Post

I am flying to San Francisco. This is the reading list for my 12-hour flight on AI Evals, Knowledge Graphs, LLMOps and Context Engineering I never had time to dig into.

Now I have no other choice. 

Here we go:

I will start with the LLMOps book by Abi Aryan ☯︎, which is probably one of the best on the topic. This one will keep me busy for a while.

Next, I will move on to some articles on evaluating RAG systems by Jason Liu:
• There are only 6 RAG Evals: https://lnkd.in/digtBsDV
• Levels of Complexity: RAG Apps: https://lnkd.in/dCNpctJN

Now, moving to one of my latest fantasies, Knowledge Graphs:
• How to Implement Graph RAG Using Knowledge Graphs and Vector Databases: https://lnkd.in/dR2m7TpU
• How to Build a Graph RAG App: https://lnkd.in/d_pHeu2p
• How to Implement Knowledge Graphs and LLMs at the Enterprise Level: https://lnkd.in/djMmu33b
• Primers • Graph Neural Networks: https://lnkd.in/dXZibfby
• Recommendation Systems • Graph Neural Networks: https://lnkd.in/dNwKupPg

Other must-reads for AI Engineers:
• Effective context engineering for AI agents: https://lnkd.in/dknFHeRF
• From Notebooks to Production Server: Build AI Applications The Right Way: https://lnkd.in/d2e6WtCk (amazing piece on applying the clean architecture to AI projects by Jeremy Arancio)
• Digging more into the nitty-gritty details of MCP. Nothing beats the FastMCP docs by Prefect on that.
• Defeating Nondeterminism in LLM Inference: https://lnkd.in/dwmgCDrr

How do I read this on a plane? Readwise Reader is an amazing app that lets you store your reading list offline while taking notes.

Probably won’t have time to read everything, but I like making lists. 

So here you go. Enjoy!

What else would you add on AI Evals, KG or LLMOps?
