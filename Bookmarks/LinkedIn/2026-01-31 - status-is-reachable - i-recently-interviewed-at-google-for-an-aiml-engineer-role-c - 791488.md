## Post

I recently interviewed at Google for an AI/ML Engineer role. Cleared the Googleyness round but got rejected in the domain interview. Sharing what I learned so it helps someone out there.

The domain interview was not what I expected. I went in thinking it would be heavy on high-level ML applications like building recommendation systems, designing complex pipelines, that sort of thing. But it was actually way more foundational than that.

Core ML concepts, Retrieval, Clustering, How and why algorithms work the way they do and not just "pick the right model" but "do you actually understand the fundamentals deeply enough to reason through problems?" Once you get through the foundational stuff, they move into how you would optimize for scale. So you need both strong basics and the ability to think about production level tradeoffs.

That caught me off guard honestly. I had prepped more on the system design and application side and not enough on the fundamentals. If I could go back, I would spend a lot more time there.

For the Googleyness round, They want to see how you handle disagreements, deal with ambiguity and work with people. Don't overthink it. Real examples from your experience go a long way. This was actually the round I enjoyed the most.
