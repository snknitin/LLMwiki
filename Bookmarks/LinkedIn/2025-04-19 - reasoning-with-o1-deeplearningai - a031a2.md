## Content

**Reasoning with o1** — DeepLearning.AI short course (Intermediate, ~1h44m; 8 video lessons, 6 code examples, 1 graded assignment).

Developed with OpenAI and taught by **Colin Jarvis** (Head of AI Solutions at OpenAI). Teaches effective prompting and application of OpenAI's o1 model, which excels at abstract reasoning (planning, coding, analysis, domain-specific reasoning like law and STEM). Covers how OpenAI used reinforcement learning to build a model that uses test-time compute, and how o1 autonomously applies chain-of-thought.

**What you'll learn**
- Identify tasks suited for o1 vs smaller/faster models
- Apply four key prompting principles, from "simple and direct" to "show rather than tell"
- Implement multi-step tasks with o1 as an orchestrator coordinating with GPT-4o-mini
- Use o1 for coding applications, testing, and development
- Apply o1 to image understanding with hierarchical reasoning
- Master meta-prompting to iteratively improve prompts

**Syllabus:** Introduction; Introduction to o1; Prompting o1; Planning with o1; Coding with o1; Reasoning with images; Meta-prompting; Conclusion; graded Quiz; Appendix.
