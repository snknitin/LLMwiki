## Post

VLMs Zero to Hero 🔥 🔥 🔥 

Vision-language models are the future! In 2024, I learned how to fine-tune them, but neglected to learn the theory behind them. Changing that is my New Year's resolution. 

This January, I'm launching a new FREE course - VLMs Zero to Hero, where I'll guide you through the core papers that paved the way to VLMs: 
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) fundamentals 
- Computer Vision (CV) fundamentals 
- Early Vision-Language models 
- Scale and Efficiency 
- Modern Vision-Language models 

The course will primarily focus on reviewing key papers, but we'll also build and train some legendary models in PyTorch from scratch. I'm already working on the first episodes about Word2Vec and Seq2Seq. 

⮑ 🔗 GitHub: https://lnkd.in/dgkKU2fe

The first videos will drop this January on my YouTube channel. Stay tuned!
