## Post

Finally, my dual DGX Spark setup is complete! 🚀

I'm running the official DeepSeek V4 Flash quantized release from DeepSeek (the official full-quality quantization, not a community-quantized version) at 60 tokens/sec decoding with a 1M-token context window, all on two boxes that fit in the palm of my hand.

DeepSeek V4 Flash is an incredible model. It's currently the most popular model on OpenRouter, and not just because it's affordable, it's because the quality is genuinely outstanding.

It's amazing how much AI inference performance now fits on a desk.
