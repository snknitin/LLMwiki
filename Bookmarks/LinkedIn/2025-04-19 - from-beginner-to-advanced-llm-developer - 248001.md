## Post

Our new course, "From Beginner to Advanced LLM Developer," contains 48 notebooks and a template repository for the final course project (because you can't learn everything and be a complete LLM developer through notebooks only).

Notebooks are really powerful to teach the core concepts quickly and easily to anyone. You don't have to set up your environment (using collab), or you can do so very easily with specific requirement packages. Still, we also believe it's important to "sweat" in order to learn, and notebooks are thus not the entirety of the course. The main goal of the course is to build your own product (deployed inside an HF space and managed on a real GitHub repository) by leveraging the techniques we teach through notebooks.

We teach every single technique in the book in those notebooks. Each one has a dedicated course lesson, which serves a specific purpose through an applied project.

We keep it super practical by teaching throughout the course to replicate our very own "AI Tutor," giving all the techniques we've ever tried (including some we didn't keep in the final version but that can be relevant). Then, the student's goal is to create and deploy their own tutor (of their choice), implementing the most advanced RAG, fine-tuning and prompting techniques, which becomes an amazing portfolio.

If, after reading this, you feel it might be a course for you, check it out here: https://lnkd.in/eP5NTpDK
