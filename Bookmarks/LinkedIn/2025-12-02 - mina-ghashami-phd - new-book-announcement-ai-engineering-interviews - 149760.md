## Post

🎉 New Book Announcement: AI Engineering Interviews!

Super excited to share that Ali Torkamani and I are writing a new book with O’Reilly: AI Engineering Interviews.

A year and half ago, as we were interviewing folks for GenAI roles, our notes started to pile up and quickly turned into a small booklet. We shared it with peers who were also in the interview cycle, and the feedback was unanimous:

 “This doesn’t exist anywhere. Let’s publish it.”
 So we did.

Fast-forward almost a year:
 👉 Three chapters are now available in Early Release on the O’Reilly platform! Link in the comment, and more chapters on the way! 



📘 What’s Inside
The entire book is written in a Q&A format.
Each chapter begins with a high-level overview to help you understand the landscape. Then we dive into a curated set of questions, the exact types of questions you’re likely to hear in interviews, followed by clear, structured answers.

To serve readers with different needs, we structured the book into two parts:

1️⃣ Practical GenAI Interviews
This part is designed to get you off the ground quickly. Here, we focus on the essentials: understanding the big picture of generative AI, working with pretrained models, mastering prompt engineering, and navigating training and fine-tuning workflows. We also cover RAG and agentic AI from a practitioner’s point of view, the way they show up in real interview loops.

This part of the book contains a lot of code, examples, and practical patterns you can apply immediately. If your interview is next week, you can start here and get up to speed fast.

2️⃣ In-Depth GenAI Interviews
This section shifts from hands-on practice to the deeper theoretical foundations behind modern AI systems. 
Here we walk through the fundamental machine learning concepts of foundation models, the inner workings of transformer architectures, how they scale to long-context settings, and more.
