LLM Fine-Tuning Course (Offered by Hugging Face)

This is a practical course on fine-tuning language models for your specific use case.

This course is offered by Hugging Face and is based on the SmolLM2 series of models.

This course covers
 1️⃣Instruction Tuning 
 2️⃣Preference Alignment 
 3️⃣Parameter-efficient Fine-tuning 
 4️⃣Evaluation 
 5️⃣Vision-language Models 
 6️⃣Synthetic Datasets
 7️⃣Inference
 8️⃣Agents
