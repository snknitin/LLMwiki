## Post

I recently completed the Reinforcement Learning for LLMs course from The RAG Pack.

This course gave me a much clearer picture of how reinforcement learning is actually used beyond theory, especially in aligning and improving large language models.

It covered everything from core ideas like rewards and policies to practical approaches such as RLHF, PPO, and GRPO.

What stood out most was the hands-on aspect.

Working through labs and analyzing training workflows made it easier to understand how models are guided to produce better, more reliable reasoning.

Really appreciate the way the instructors broke down complex topics into something easy to follow.

Thanks to Jay Alammar, Maarten Grootendorst, Luis Serrano, Joshua Starmer PhD, and Chris McCormick for putting this together.

Looking forward to applying these learnings in real-world problems.
