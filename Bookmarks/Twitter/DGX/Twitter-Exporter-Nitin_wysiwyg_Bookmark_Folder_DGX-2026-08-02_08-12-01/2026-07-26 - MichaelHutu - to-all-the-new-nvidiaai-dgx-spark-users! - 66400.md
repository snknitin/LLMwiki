If you have just bought and setup your first DGX-Spark, this is something that you should know sooner than later. 

When you try AI models and test the limits of this tiny little powerhouse up to OOM (Out-Of-Memory), the machine can get in to a state that limits the power. 

This will nerf down every single recipe you try, and it is not because  you do something wrong, it is because you need to boot the machine properly. 

If your DGX-spark stay in 5w-15w and 650Mhz under load, your machine is in that state. 

What you need to do is very simple:
bash
Sudo shutdown -> wait for the machine to shutdown
-> Unplug the power cable and wait for 1min -> Plug it back and voila, back to full speed.
