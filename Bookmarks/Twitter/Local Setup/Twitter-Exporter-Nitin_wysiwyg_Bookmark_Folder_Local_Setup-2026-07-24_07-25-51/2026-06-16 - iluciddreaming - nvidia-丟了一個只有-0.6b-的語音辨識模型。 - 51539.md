# NVIDIA 丟了一個只有 0.6B 的語音辨識模型。

> **Source:** [@iluciddreaming](https://x.com/iluciddreaming) · 2026-06-16 · 👍 5138 · 💬 70 · 🔖 6536 · 👁 378669

> 🔗 [View tweet on X](https://x.com/iluciddreaming/status/2066769543943651539)

## Tweet Content

NVIDIA dropped a speech recognition model with just 0.6B parameters.

It's called Nemotron-3.5-ASR.

It supports over 40 languages, with real-time streaming output.

It can run purely on CPU, no GPU required.

Its speed is 2.5 times that of the official Nemo runtime, yet the recognition results are completely identical.

You can use it directly in offline environments and seamlessly integrate it into your agent pipeline.

For speech processing, local agents now have another small and fast option.
