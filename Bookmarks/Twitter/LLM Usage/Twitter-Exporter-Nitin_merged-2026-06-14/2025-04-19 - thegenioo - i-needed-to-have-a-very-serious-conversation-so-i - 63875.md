So I gave o3 a full fair try and its responses were very good but not what I needed or tailored for me or my situation, it was ignoring very important details and a very important main aspect of the whole conversation.

I did not plan this because it was very serious for me and very important matter so I just out of curiosity gave Gemini 2.5 Pro a try just in case. I am just blown away by how impressive its responses were. It heard me way more than o3 even tried to focus on.

Its solution was realistic something actually sensible, and a bit harsh but practical which should be done rather than how o3 was making me run away from the situation somehow.
