How to use LLMs without giving your private data away? 

𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁'𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗹𝗲𝗺?
 
Behind every LLM app, no matter how complex, agentic or ragish, there is at least one LLM that acts as either/and

> the planer
> tool decider
> output synthetizer.

And let's face it.

Using third-party LLM providers like OpenAI, Claude or Grok is ok when you are building a demo.

However, in the industry, most companies I have worked for, do not trust these LLM providers.

Not event the self-proclaimed super safe AWS Bedrock serve.

So, what are the options.

𝗦𝗼𝗹𝘂𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀

Managing LLM servers means you need GPUs.
GPUs connected to the rest of services of your infra.

Now, the most cost-effective way to do so is IMHO Kubernetes.

Kubernetes, and on top of it, one of these 2 serving layers:

> vLLM -> one of the first of its kind, with good Kubernetes support.

> NVIDIA NIM -> a collection of super-optimized Docker containers for inference on NVIDIA GPUs, the dominant hardware provider in the market.

In the following weeks Marius Rugan and myself will break down the process of running LLMs at scale without breaking the bank.
