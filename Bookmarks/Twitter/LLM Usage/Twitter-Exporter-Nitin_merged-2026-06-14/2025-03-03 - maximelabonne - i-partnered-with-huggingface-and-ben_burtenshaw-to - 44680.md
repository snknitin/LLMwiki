# I partnered with @huggingface  and @ben_burtenshaw  to teach people how to fine-tune LLMs with GRPO.

## Tweet Content

I partnered with 
@huggingface
  and 
@ben_burtenshaw
  to teach people how to fine-tune LLMs with GRPO.

In this notebook, we fine-tune a tiny SmolLM-135M model on my filtered smoltldr dataset. Thanks to our reward function, we encourage the model to output generations ("TL;DR") of around 50 characters.

Interestingly, this 135M model can learn this behavior with only 2k samples! The quality of the final results varies, but it was a fascinating exercise to tweak hyperparameters and get an intuition of how these tiny models behave with GRPO.

It's been a fun collaboration, I hope you'll enjoy the course. :)
