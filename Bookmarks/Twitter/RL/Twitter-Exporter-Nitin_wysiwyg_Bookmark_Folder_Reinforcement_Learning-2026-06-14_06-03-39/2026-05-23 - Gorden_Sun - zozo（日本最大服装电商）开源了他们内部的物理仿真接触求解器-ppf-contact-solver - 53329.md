# ZOZO（日本最大服装电商）开源了他们内部的物理仿真接触求解器 ppf-contact-solver。

> **Source:** [@Gorden_Sun](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun) · 2026-05-23 · 👍 6165 · 💬 123 · 🔖 4165 · 👁 661396

> 🔗 [View tweet on X](https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2058208360944853329)

## Tweet Content

ZOZO (Japan's largest clothing e-commerce platform) has open-sourced their internal physics simulation contact solver ppf-contact-solver.  
This tool is specifically designed to address collision and contact issues for fabrics, ropes, and soft bodies in simulations, ensuring complete non-penetration and strict fabric stretching limits (no clipping like in games), capable of handling over 180 million contact points in a single scene, and includes a Blender plugin.
