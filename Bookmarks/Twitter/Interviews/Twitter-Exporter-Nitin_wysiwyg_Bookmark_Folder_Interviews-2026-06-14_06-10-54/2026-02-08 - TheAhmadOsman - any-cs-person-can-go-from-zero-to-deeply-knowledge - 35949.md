# any cs person can go from zero to deeply knowledgeable in llms and ai in ~2 years, top to bottom

> **Source:** [@TheAhmadOsman](https://x.com/TheAhmadOsman) · 2026-02-08 · 👍 973 · 💬 27 · 🔖 1403 · 👁 44982

> 🔗 [View tweet on X](https://x.com/TheAhmadOsman/status/2020433115584335949)

## Tweet Content

any cs person can go from zero to deeply knowledgeable in llms and ai in ~2 years, top to bottom

key topics on how llms work:

> tokenization and embeddings
> positional embeddings (absolute, rope, alibi)
> self attention and multihead attention
> transformers
> qkv
> sampling params: temperature, top-k top-p
> kv cache (and why inference is fast)
> infini attention & sliding window (long context tricks)
> mixture of experts (moe routing layers)
> grouped query attention
> normalization and activations
> pretraining objectives (causal, masked, etc)
> finetuning vs instruction tuning vs rlhf
> scaling laws and model capacity curves

bonus topics:

> quantizations - qat vs ptq (ggufs, awq, etc)
> training vs inference stacks (deepspeed, vllm, etc)
> synthetic data generation

the elite don't want you to know this
