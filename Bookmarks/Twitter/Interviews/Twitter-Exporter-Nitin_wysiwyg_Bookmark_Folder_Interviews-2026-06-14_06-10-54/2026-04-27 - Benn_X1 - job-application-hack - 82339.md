## Tweet Content

Job application hack:

Save your CV as 001-[firstname]-[lastname].pdf

This will force the downloaded CV to have a high rank in the recruiter's download folder. It will most likely be the first file in the folder.
I used this a lot back in the day. Same name format for cover letters too.
