## Tweet Content

Implementing direct preference optimization (DPO) is so much more convenient than RLHF with PPO for LLM preference tuning. Can we go even simpler? Apparently, yes: "SimPO: Simple Preference Optimization with a Reference-Free Reward (
https://
arxiv.org/abs/2405.14734). 
I.e., remove the need for a reference model and use the average log probability of a sequence as the implicit reward.
