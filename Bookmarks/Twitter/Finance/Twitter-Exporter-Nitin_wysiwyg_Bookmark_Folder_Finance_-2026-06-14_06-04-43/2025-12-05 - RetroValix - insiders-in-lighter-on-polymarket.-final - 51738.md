> 
> > He will want to hide his identity so that there are no questions for him
> 
> For this he creates a new wallet and registers a new Polymarket account with it. Then he makes a deposit to this account from a wallet or from a card. Or he can create a Polymarket account with email and make a deposit there from a card or from another wallet. In this way, with regular tools it is impossible to track the identity of the insider and his connections with the market on which he makes a prediction.
> 
> > He does not hide his identity
>

>

> 
> > These guys created a Polymarket account recently

> > They made a prediction on the market where they have information. And in parallel they made prediction on markets where the probability of the outcome is 99 - 100%

> > They made a prediction on the market where they have information and in parallel are engaged in wash trading: they buy and immediately sell other markets
