# We’re releasing TranslateGemma, a new family of open translation models with support for 55 language...

> **Source:** [@GoogleDeepMind](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind) · 2026-01-15 · 👍 6846 · 💬 191 · 🔖 3780 · 👁 2188015

> 🔗 [View tweet on X](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2011848249850630363)

## Tweet Content

We’re releasing TranslateGemma, a new family of open translation models with support for 55 languages. 

Available in 4B, 12B, and 27B parameter sizes – they’re designed for efficiency without sacrificing quality.

Built on Gemma 3, TranslateGemma was trained on data generated by Gemini – effectively transferring its intelligence into a smaller package.

This means developers can build low-latency translation tools that run entirely on-device.
