# The biggest thing I use LLMs for now is to drop a voice note of what I'm thinking for a section with...

> **Source:** [@LoganJensen1](https://x.com/LoganJensen1) · 2025-05-05 · 👍 1 · 💬 0 · 🔖 1 · 👁 85

> 🔗 [View tweet on X](https://x.com/LoganJensen1/status/1919275745194692990)

## Tweet Content

The biggest thing I use LLMs for now is to drop a voice note of what I'm thinking for a section with some reference material and then let the thing drafty the section from my thoughts, the material, and some filler to check and approve before moving onto the next. I go from thought to completed section in a few minutes and it tends to be 90% at least as good as what I'd do if I sat down and took an hour to write out the whole section by hand.

I'm glad doctors are using the same, or similar, workflow to actually document things in reports that are much more consequential.
