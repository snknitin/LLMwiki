# Attended an interview today, and honestly I don't know what role I was being evaluated for. The recr...

> **Source:** [@initlayers](https://x.com/initlayers) · 2025-12-30 · 👍 443 · 💬 34 · 🔖 687 · 👁 43733

> 🔗 [View tweet on X](https://x.com/initlayers/status/2006032225087938982)

## Tweet Content

Attended an interview today, and honestly I don't know what role I was being evaluated for. The recruiter said that it was just a resume screening round, but fun twist, the interviewer never checked my resume and directly started firing ML questions.

Questions asked (Core ML):
1) What is a decision tree? how does it work?
2) Difference between decision trees and boosting.
3) What is logistic regression? Why do we use sigmoid hypothesis?
4) Why not use softmax instead of sigmoid?
5) What is K-means clustering?
6) What is the difference between K-means and KNN?
7) If I give you a point and K-means predicts a cluster for it, will KNN also predict the same cluster?

Related to my research:
8) How many models did you train, is it a single model, or a cluster?
9) What are the modalities you dealt with?

Questions asked (CNN/DL):
10) What is a filter? Why is it used?
11) What is a kernel? (Just to check)
12) What is the effect of increasing the kernel size on training?
13) If we are capturing hand moments using an CNN, I started with a 4×4 kernel and increased it till 32×32 where I found the best result yet, should I go ahead with 64×64?
14) What is max pooling?
15) Why not use min pooling over max pooling?
16) Explain a bit about attention mechanism?
17) Why not use attention with max pooling?
18) What is attention pooling? Is it better over traditional max pooling?
19) What is GeLU, and where do we use it?

It was for a Bangalore-based start-up. I answered almost all of these, so fingers crossed. If things go well, the next round is an assignment.
