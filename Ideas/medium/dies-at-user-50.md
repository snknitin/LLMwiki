# Dies at User 50

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · DODO PAYMENTS

## Brief

Drop your vibe-coded app's URL and a swarm of AI test users piles in one at a time until something breaks. You get the Survived User 50 badge or a death certificate reading Died at user 37, cause: unhandled error at checkout, plus a public leaderboard of every app tested.
