# Price My Room

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** LINKUP · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · DODO PAYMENTS

## Brief

Upload one photo of your room and get it back with a price tag floating on everything, laptop, chair, the guitar you never play, plus the total: your room liquidates for 2.1 lakh rupees. Priced from live resale listings, not guesses.
