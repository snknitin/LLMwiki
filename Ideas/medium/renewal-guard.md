# Renewal Guard

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Revenue  
**Partners:** DODO PAYMENTS · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

Forward any receipt, invoice, or subscription email to your Renewal Guard address and it builds your vault: every warranty and auto-renewal tracked, with WhatsApp pings before money leaks and a claims walkthrough when something breaks.
