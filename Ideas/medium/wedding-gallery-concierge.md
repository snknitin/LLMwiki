# Wedding Gallery Concierge

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Revenue  
**Partners:** DODO PAYMENTS · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

Photographers drop a delivered wedding gallery; the couple and guests text a bot in plain language, all candids of grandma at the haldi, and get the right photos back. Photographers pay per event and resell it as a premium add-on.
