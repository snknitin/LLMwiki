# Instant Quote Machine

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Revenue  
**Partners:** CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · DODO PAYMENTS

## Brief

An instant-quote lead magnet for local services like painters, movers, and interior work: a homeowner answers five questions and uploads photos, gets a ballpark quote in 60 seconds, and the business gets a qualified lead with photos, scope, and a drafted callback.
