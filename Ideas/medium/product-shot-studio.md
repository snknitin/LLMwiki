# Product Shot Studio

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Revenue  
**Partners:** DODO PAYMENTS · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

Small sellers send one phone photo of their product to a WhatsApp or Telegram bot and get back a marketplace-ready pack in minutes: clean cutout, lifestyle scene, and an ad creative with copy, paid per pack.
