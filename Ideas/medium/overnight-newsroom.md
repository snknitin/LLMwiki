# Overnight Newsroom

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** AI as Agency  
**Partners:** ELEVENLABS · CLOUDFLARE · CONVEX · LINKUP

## Brief

An autonomous newsroom that ships broadcast-quality audio bulletins from live Indian feeds with no human trigger: a monitor watches the wires, an editor picks the rundown, a writer scripts it, a judge fact-gates every line, anchor voices read it, and a publisher posts to a public feed and Telegram channel.
