# Website in a WhatsApp

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** CLOUDFLARE · CONVEX · DODO PAYMENTS · ELEVENLABS

## Brief

A bot that turns a chat thread into a live website: text your business details, photos, and a voice note to WhatsApp or Telegram, get a deployed site on your own link in minutes, then edit it forever by just texting changes.
