# Daily Parent Check-In

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Revenue  
**Partners:** DODO PAYMENTS · ELEVENLABS · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

Adult children subscribe; the agent makes a warm daily check-in with their parent on WhatsApp in their language, remembers their stories, and sends the child a short evening digest with anything worth worrying about.
