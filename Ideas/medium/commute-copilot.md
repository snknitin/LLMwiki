# Commute Copilot

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** CLOUDFLARE · CONVEX · LINKUP

## Brief

A city transit bot: subscribe your daily route in one message and get live departures, disruption alerts before you leave the house, and reroutes when things break. Every alert is a clean shareable card your whole office group forwards.
