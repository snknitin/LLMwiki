# Rabbit Hole Speedrun

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** LINKUP · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

Users start at any topic and race down a chain of connected facts, each wilder than the last; when they quit, their rabbit hole path becomes a share card with a depth score.
