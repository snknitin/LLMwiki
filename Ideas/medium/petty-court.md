# Petty Court

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** ELEVENLABS · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · DODO PAYMENTS

## Brief

Small claims court for petty fights: both parties submit their side and evidence, an AI judge cross-examines each separately, then delivers a spoken verdict and a shareable ruling card. Every ruling becomes public precedent that future litigants can cite.
