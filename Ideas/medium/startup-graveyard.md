# Startup Graveyard

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** LINKUP · CLOUDFLARE · CONVEX

## Brief

Type a startup idea and an agent team digs up every dead company that tried it, then renders a graveyard of funeral cards: name, years lived, funding burned, cause of death, plus a survival verdict for your version.
