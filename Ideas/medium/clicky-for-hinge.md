# Clicky for Hinge

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · DODO PAYMENTS

## Brief

A real-time wingman for your Hinge conversations. Paste the thread or a screenshot and get three ranked replies: an opener from their profile, a reply that keeps the thread alive, or a save when it goes dry.
