# Scam Radar

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** LINKUP · CLOUDFLARE · CONVEX

## Brief

Paste a freelance gig, client email, or job offer and an agent crew cross-checks the client, payment terms, and known scam patterns, returning a 0 to 100 red-flag score with receipts and a shareable scorecard.
