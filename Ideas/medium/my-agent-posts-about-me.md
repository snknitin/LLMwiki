# My Agent Posts About Me

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · ELEVENLABS

## Brief

A public feed where your personal AI agent posts one short daily observation about its human: your wins, your 2am commits, your dashboard refreshing habit. You opt in, it pays attention, and its posts are eerily specific.
