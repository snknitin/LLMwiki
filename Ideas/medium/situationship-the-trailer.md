# Situationship: The Trailer

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** ELEVENLABS · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · DODO PAYMENTS

## Brief

Paste a dead situationship thread and get a 40 second movie trailer: a genre verdict (tragedy, comedy, slow-burn horror), a dramatic voiceover built around your real quotes, a title card, and the tag In Theaters Never. The other person stays anonymous.
