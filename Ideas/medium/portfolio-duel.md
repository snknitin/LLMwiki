# Portfolio Duel

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** CONVEX · ELEVENLABS · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

Two friends drop their portfolio or GitHub links into a versus arena and get a head-to-head scorecard that crowns a winner per category, with a challenge link to keep the chain going.
