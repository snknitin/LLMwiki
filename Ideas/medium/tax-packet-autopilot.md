# Tax Packet Autopilot

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Revenue  
**Partners:** CONVEX · DODO PAYMENTS · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

For freelancers and small firms: connect a mailbox and the agent hunts every invoice and receipt PDF, extracts vendor, GST, and amount, categorizes them, chases clients for missing months, and outputs a clean CA-ready packet.
