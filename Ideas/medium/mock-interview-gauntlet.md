# Mock Interview Gauntlet

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Track:** Revenue  
**Partners:** ELEVENLABS · DODO PAYMENTS · LINKUP · CONVEX

## Brief

Paste a job description and your resume; a voice interviewer runs a 20 minute adaptive mock, pushes back on weak answers, then replays your three worst fumbles and re-drills each until you pass.
