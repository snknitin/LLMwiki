# Podcast Clips Agency

**Difficulty:** HARD  
**Track:** AI as Agency  
**Partners:** CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · LINKUP

## Brief

Drop a podcast episode and an agent crew ships it: a transcriber, a moment scorer, parallel clip cutters, a captioner, a thumbnail artist, and a publisher that posts the top clips to the show's real social accounts.
