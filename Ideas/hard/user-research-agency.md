# User Research Agency

**Difficulty:** HARD  
**Track:** AI as Agency  
**Partners:** ELEVENLABS · CONVEX · LINKUP · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

Give it a research question and it runs the whole study: a recruiter agent finds and screens respondents, interviewer agents run structured 10 minute interviews in parallel, an analyst clusters the transcripts, and a writer publishes a findings report with real quotes.
