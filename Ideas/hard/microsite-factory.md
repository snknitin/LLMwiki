# Microsite Factory

**Difficulty:** HARD  
**Track:** AI as Agency  
**Partners:** CLOUDFLARE · LINKUP · CONVEX

## Brief

Paste a list of 20 target accounts and an agency of agents ships a personalized microsite for each: a researcher digs the account, a strategist picks the angle, a builder generates the page in their language, QA screenshots and gates, and a deployer pushes each to its own live URL.
