# On-Call Autopilot

**Difficulty:** HARD  
**Track:** AI as Agency  
**Partners:** ELEVENLABS · CLOUDFLARE · CONVEX · LINKUP

## Brief

An AI on-call engineer: point it at a broken deploy and it reads logs, names the root cause, patches config or code, redeploys, verifies the fix with a real request, and opens the PR, narrated over voice.
