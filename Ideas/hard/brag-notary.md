# Brag Notary

**Difficulty:** HARD  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** LINKUP · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

Users submit a brag like 'I hit 10k users' plus evidence links, and get a notarized Verified Brag badge with the evidence trail, or a public Unverified stamp if it does not hold up.
