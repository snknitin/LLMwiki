# Edge Desk

**Difficulty:** HARD  
**Track:** Revenue  
**Partners:** LINKUP · CONVEX · DODO PAYMENTS · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

A prediction-market intelligence desk: parallel agents watch Polymarket order books, breaking news, and big-wallet moves, spot markets whose odds lag reality, and sell the evidence-backed alerts as a subscription feed. Read-only public APIs, no trading account needed.
