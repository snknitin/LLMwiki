# Winback Agency

**Difficulty:** HARD  
**Track:** AI as Agency  
**Partners:** CONVEX · LINKUP · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

A customer winback agency as an agent crew: a segmenter mines a business's lapsed customer list, a copywriter drafts per-segment offers in brand voice, a sender runs the WhatsApp and email campaign, and a reply handler books orders or logs objections.
