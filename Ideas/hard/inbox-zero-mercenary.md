# Inbox Zero Mercenary

**Difficulty:** HARD  
**Track:** AI as Agency  
**Partners:** CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · ELEVENLABS

## Brief

An agent crew you point at a drowning inbox: it triages thousands of emails, unsubscribes from junk, archives dead threads, drafts replies for the ones that matter, and logs every action it takes.
