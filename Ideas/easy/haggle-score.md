# Haggle Score

**Difficulty:** EASY  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** ELEVENLABS · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

A chat game where you haggle with an AI bazaar shopkeeper over a real item and get a shareable Haggle Score card with a beat-my-price challenge link.
