# Fine Print Rage Meter

**Difficulty:** EASY  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · LINKUP

## Brief

Users paste any terms of service or privacy policy and get the five most outrageous clauses translated into plain English with a 0 to 100 rage score.
