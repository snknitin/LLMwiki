# Meet in the Middle

**Difficulty:** EASY  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** LINKUP · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

A WhatsApp bot that ends the where-do-we-meet thread: everyone in the group drops a location pin, it computes the fair midpoint and pitches three venues with honest reasons, and the group votes right in chat.
