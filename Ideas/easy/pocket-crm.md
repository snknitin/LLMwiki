# Pocket CRM

**Difficulty:** EASY  
**Track:** Revenue  
**Partners:** CONVEX · DODO PAYMENTS · ELEVENLABS

## Brief

A WhatsApp-native CRM for freelancers: text it after every call, like met Priya, wants a logo, follow up Tuesday. The agent maintains the pipeline, nudges you before deals go cold, and drafts the follow-up message for each stale lead.
