# NPC Mode

**Difficulty:** EASY  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE · ELEVENLABS

## Brief

Go NPC for a day: your agent makes every decision, what to eat, when to work, whether to reply to that text, delivered as Telegram prompts you simply obey. Your day streams to a public NPC Mode log page, and you end with a recap card scoring your obedience.
