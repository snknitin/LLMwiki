# Latte Into Lambo

**Difficulty:** EASY  
**Track:** Virality  
**Partners:** LINKUP · CONVEX · CLOUDFLARE

## Brief

Users paste a past purchase and its date, like 'iPhone 13, Sept 2021', and get a card showing what that money would be worth today in NVDA, BTC, or an index fund.
