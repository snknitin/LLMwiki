# "I vibe-coded my TV" wave, Oct 16-21 2026: catalogue

Read-only research, 2026-10-21. Sources: the Azadux roundup thread (all 7 numbered entries + 32 replies), the quoted tweets' own threads, the four named posts, X searches for Fire TV / Bravia / Projectivy, and the GitHub READMEs (read via `gh api`, nothing executed).

Owner's devices: **Fire TV Stick on a Sanyo TV** (the Sanyo panel is irrelevant; what matters is the Stick's Fire OS version) and **Sony Bravia Google TV**.

## The short version

- Almost every hack is the same recipe: Developer options -> USB/network debugging -> a coding agent on a PC on the same Wi-Fi runs `adb` -> `pm disable-user` on ads/telemetry packages (reversible, nothing uninstalled) -> sideload a custom launcher APK -> take over the Home key.
- **Google TV (Sony Bravia):** full recipe works. Sony is the 2nd best-tested brand in CleanYourTV (22 Bravia lists, incl. Google TV 12/14 models). Gotcha: do not disable Google TV's stock launcher (`launcherx`) or the setup wizard (`setupwraith`) blindly; take Home via an accessibility service instead.
- **Fire TV Stick:** debloat works. Fire OS will not let you set a different default launcher, so every Fire TV launcher uses an **accessibility service that sees Amazon's home open and jumps to the custom one** (Home on Fire, Projectivy, fire-tv-web-launcher, Orchard, Fire TV Glass). Amazon's home can flash for a moment, a long-press of Home still gets you Amazon's home, and Fire OS updates can break the trick. Fire TV Stick 2nd gen (Fire OS 5) can't run these. Alternative: take over the **screensaver** instead of the launcher (xenappblog).
- A PC is needed once, to install. After that the TV runs on its own (poly-tv-odds and CleanYourTV both say so). TidyTV (an Android phone app) does the debloat with no PC.

## Warnings people raised

- **OTA undoes it:** "OTA updates will reinstall those packages and may overwrite your custom launcher" (Athaxv05). Others asked "does the debloat survive a firmware update" (jamalavedra, drefrajo). CleanYourTV's `check.py --fix` re-applies the list; many lists also disable the updater, which is risky both ways. fire-tv-web-launcher: "A Fire OS update can stop the Home button method from working." srepdor gotcha G2: the TV silently drops the accessibility service after app/firmware updates, so his launcher re-adds itself using `WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS`.
- **Bricked or looping launcher:** on Google TV, disabling `launcherx` makes setupwraith re-enable it or crash-loop Home (CleanYourTV, srepdor G1). Disabling setupwraith can delete the saved Wi-Fi network and **break phone casting** on Chromecast/GTV Streamer. Keep the stock launcher as a fallback and add a crash guard (sebuzdugan -> srepdor). nathanclark: "may very well brick your TV"; tomic_mihael got a warning about taking over Home on a Frame and backed off. Opening a CVTE/Haier hidden factory menu can kill Input/CEC and disable Play services and Netflix at the next boot.
- **Warranty:** "This may void your warranty" (evolvedentity); "does it void warranty?" (Dhruv14588676). Nobody gave an answer. ADB disabling is not rooting, but the LineageOS route (Pumpitito) clearly is.
- **Netflix / DRM:** no reply reported Netflix breaking after a normal debloat. DRM shows up as: screenshots go black over DRM video (poly-tv-odds, CleanYourTV), ClearTV can't preview Prime because it is DRM-protected, and the factory-menu case disables Netflix. Sva exposes Widevine settings for IPTV.
- **Fire TV limits:** Fire OS refuses a third-party default Home. Amazon's home flashes for a moment, long-press Home always escapes to it, and force-stopping the launcher kills its Home service. Fire TV Stick 2nd gen / Fire OS 5 is unsupported (and `pm` is refused on Fire OS 5). One user's TCL set was "blocked by Amazon".
- **Model refusals:** several people got Claude to refuse (lucataco, theBuoyantMan on LG root, JustPlainOrange "copyright", joakimhi "blocked for cyber reasons"); others asked "how did you convince Claude". Framing it as a reversible debloat of a TV you own (CleanYourTV's PROMPT.md) generally works.
- **Security/privacy:** the ADB-over-Wi-Fi port stays open, so turn debugging off afterwards (poly-tv-odds says the overlay keeps working). Notification-listener permission is broad (poly-tv-odds says its listener reads only the media session). Ridicule of putting all-time revenue on the living-room screen.
- **Parental control isn't real:** JoshDaws said that needs root.
- **Google TV "basic mode" / Basic TV as a no-ADB option:** **not raised** in any thread or search I read (one search for "basic tv" returned nothing). It is background knowledge, not from the sources: Google TV sets (Sony included) offer "Basic TV" at setup or after a reset, which removes the recommendation/ad home but also most apps. That makes it useless if you stream.

## For the owner's two devices

- **Sony Bravia Google TV:** best fit. Option A, CleanYourTV (check `examples/sony-*` for your exact model; 22 lists). Option B, keep the stock launcher and use an accessibility-Home launcher (Rivi, barehome, 3D Home TV OS). The custom-ideas path is a fork from the SHOWCASE: dashboard screensaver, weather sky, Home Assistant panel. Overlays à la poly-tv-odds work here (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`).
- **Fire TV Stick (Sanyo):** first check the Stick generation and Fire OS version (Settings -> My Fire TV -> About). On 4K / 4K Max / Lite (Fire OS 7/8): orchard-tv detox + launcher, or fire-tv-web-launcher (the home screen is a web page, easiest to make into a doooyle-style dashboard), or the screensaver takeover (xenappblog). ClearTV is a no-launcher alternative.
