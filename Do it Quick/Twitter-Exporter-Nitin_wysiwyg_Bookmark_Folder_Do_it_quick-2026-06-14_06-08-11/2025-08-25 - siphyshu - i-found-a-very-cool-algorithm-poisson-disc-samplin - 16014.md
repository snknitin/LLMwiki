i found a very cool algorithm: poisson disc sampling

it lets you place objects in a random but uniform & natural looking way

it's surprising how often this algorithm shows up where you'd expect plain old .random() to work just fine

it's widely used in gamedev for procedural object placement (trees, enemies, props) and computer graphics. i discovered it when i needed to place a bunch of assets scattered on a BG layer
