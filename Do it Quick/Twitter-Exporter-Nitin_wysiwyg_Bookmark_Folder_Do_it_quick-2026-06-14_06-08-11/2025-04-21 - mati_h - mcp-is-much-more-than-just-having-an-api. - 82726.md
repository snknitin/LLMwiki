# MCP is much more than just having an API.

> **Source:** [@mati_h](https://x.com/mati_h) · 2025-04-21 · 👍 187 · 💬 9 · 🔖 136 · 👁 42965

> 🔗 [View tweet on X](https://x.com/mati_h/status/1914425736741482726)

## Tweet Content

MCP is much more than just having an API.

MCP enables sharing resources and prompts, plus subscribing to events.

Consider a pizza store  with 5-10 endpoints:

- Choose Delivery location
- Choose Delivery time
- Get available toppings
- Discount logic (buy 2, get the 3rd free)
And more

An LLM like Claude can't inherently understand the correct ordering process, e.g. is the delivery time required before choosing a location?

With MCP, the pizza store can share a prompt with detailed instructions on the ordering process.

This allows an LLM to follow the correct procedure accurately without any prior knowledge.
