## Tweet Content

Here's a great ML interview question: 

First ask about regularization. The candidate will likely talk about norm penalties on weights.

Then ask "why do we do lambda norm penalties, - lambda|w| , instead of directly constraining |w|<R if they're essentially equivalent under KKT?"

There are three really nice perspectives 

1. Optimization theory 
2. Bayesian theory 
3. ER learning theory
