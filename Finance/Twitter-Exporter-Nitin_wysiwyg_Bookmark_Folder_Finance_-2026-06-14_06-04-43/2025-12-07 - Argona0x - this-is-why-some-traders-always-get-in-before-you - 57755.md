# this is why some traders always get in before you do

## Tweet Content

this is why some traders always get in before you do

the average polymarket user reads a tweet, checks a news site, and only after that puts in their bet

i was trying to figure out how to speed things up, and came across something worth sharing

there's this open source tool called polymarket-mcp-server that connects claude (the AI) directly to polymarket's API

in simple terms, it turns claude into a trading assistant that can:

> watch live prices in real-time via websocket
> scan news the second it drops
> check order books before you place big bets
> tell you if slippage gonna eat your profits

let's say some company announces a new CEO at 2pm humans read the headline, think about it, open polymarket, find the market, place the bet

takes maybe 5-10 minutes if you're fast

AI sees the announcement instantly, processes what it means, checks if the polymarket odds are still old (when the odds show 30% but the news basically screams 60%), and flags it for you

you get in before the crowd adjusts the price

///

the tool has 45+ functions built in

you can ask claude stuff like "what's the current liquidity on this market" or "show me markets with volume over 100k" and it just pulls the data

the tool is free, open source, and takes around 30 minutes to install if you're comfortable with basic python

but that doesn't mean you instantly win 
you still have to read the markets yourself
