# There’s a hidden Polymarket mechanic that 99%+ of traders have no idea about

> **Source:** [@crydevil_crypto](https://x.com/crydevil_crypto) · 2025-12-12 · 👍 167 · 💬 32 · 🔖 178 · 👁 21378

## Tweet Content

There’s a hidden Polymarket mechanic that 99%+ of traders have no idea about

Basically, if none of the listed 'YES' options win, the victory automatically goes to 'Other'

(this happened with the "Time 2025 Person of the Year" market)

But the catch is: you can’t just go and buy 'Other'

For example, if you open the “Democratic Presidential Nominee 2028” market


https://
polymarket.com/event/democrat
ic-presidential-nominee-2028?via=lucky
…

(or any similar one where there are tons of candidates but only 1 winner)

by default you won’t see 'Other' as a separate option:

To actually get shares in this “mystery” candidate, you need to:

> take some unpopular option, like “NO” on MrBeast
> use Convert 

that way you end up with YES shares for every outcome, including 'Other'

Now if new candidates appear in the market, I’ll automatically get YES shares for them.

You won't need this mechanic in most markets, but it's important to keep it in mind for rare cases like the TIME market and be prepared for unlikely outcomes.
